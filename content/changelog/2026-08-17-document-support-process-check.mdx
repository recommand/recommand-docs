---
title: Check document support per process
description: The verify document support endpoint now accepts a process identifier, and the company document types endpoint lists every format Recommand can receive on your behalf.
date: 2026-08-17
---

Some document types are published for more than one process - French invoices, for example, travel over a regulated and a non-regulated process. Recipient checks and receiving registrations now take the process into account.

### What changed

- **`processId` on verify document support**: Pass an optional `processId`, with or without its scheme prefix, to the [verify document support endpoint](/reference/recipients/verify-document-support) to check whether a recipient published that document type for that specific process. When omitted, any published process is accepted, as before.
- **Process checked before sending**: Outgoing documents are checked against the process they are actually sent over, so a mismatch is reported up front instead of failing at the recipient.
- **Receiving capabilities listed**: The [list company document types endpoint](/reference/company-document-types/get-company-document-types) now also returns a `receivingCapabilities` array with every format Recommand can receive, including its title, `docTypeId` and `processId`, so you can register the right combinations for a company.

### Why this matters

Publishing or verifying a document type without its process can send a document over a process the recipient never registered. The additional field makes that check exact, and the capability list removes the guesswork from registering document types.
