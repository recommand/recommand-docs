date: 2026-10-24
---

Not every e-invoicing mandate requires Peppol. In Germany, from 2027 (2028 for smaller companies), an XRechnung or Peppol BIS invoice sent by email counts as an e-invoice, and a PDF does not. Many German businesses are not on Peppol yet. You can now send them a compliant e-invoice through the same API call you already use.

### What you can do now

- **Email an e-invoice instead of a PDF.** For a buyer you know is not on Peppol, leave out the Peppol recipient and choose the format, for example XRechnung. Recommand writes the invoice in that format, checks it against the official rules before anything is sent, and emails it with the XML attached, together with your PDF if you include one. The invoice is kept with your other documents, XML included.
- **Use Peppol where you can, email where you can't.** Address the buyer on Peppol, for a German company by its VAT number, and add an email address. When the buyer is on Peppol, the invoice goes over Peppol. When it is not, the email with the XML goes out straight away, and the document shows that the buyer was not found on Peppol. One flow covers all your customers, without checking first who is on Peppol.

Sending a PDF by email, for example to consumers, works as before.

### Good to know

- A Peppol BIS invoice needs the buyer's VAT number or company number. For a buyer without one, choose XRechnung, which can use the buyer's email address instead.
- French regulated invoices still go over Peppol through an accredited platform. They cannot be sent by email only.

### For developers

- Set `doctypeId` on a send with `recipient: null` to email a structured document. If you already set it on email-only sends, those are now validated, and a Peppol BIS invoice for a buyer without a VAT or company number is refused with a `400`. Leave `doctypeId` out to keep sending a PDF only.
- A send to an address that is not on Peppol now fails with category `recipient_not_found` instead of `transport`.

The [email delivery guide](/docs/email-delivery-and-notifications#structured-documents-by-email) and the [German getting started guide](/getting-started/germany) show both ways in detail.
