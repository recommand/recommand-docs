A structured e-invoice does not have to travel over Peppol. In Germany, where domestic B2B invoices have to be structured e-invoices from 2027 (2028 for smaller companies), an XRechnung or Peppol BIS XML delivered by email counts, and a PDF does not. Many buyers are not on Peppol yet. Until now an email-only send carried a PDF only, and a send to an address that was not on Peppol at all first went through a transmission that could only fail.

### What changed

- **Structured documents by email**: On a send with `recipient: null`, set `doctypeId` (and, if needed, `processId`). The document is written in that format, validated as it would be for a Peppol recipient, stored with the document, and attached to the email as XML. A document that fails validation is refused with a `400` listing the failing rules. Without `doctypeId`, nothing changes: the email carries the PDF.
- **Buyer's electronic address**: Such a document needs the buyer's electronic address (BT-49). It is taken from the buyer's enterprise number under its scheme, else its VAT number, else the first address in `email.to`. Peppol BIS 3 does not accept an email address there, so a Peppol BIS document for a buyer with neither number is refused; XRechnung and CII accept it. On self-billing documents the seller's numbers are used.
- **French regulated documents** cannot be delivered by email only.
- **Unregistered Peppol recipients**: When the Peppol network says a recipient is not registered, no transmission is attempted. The Peppol delivery fails with category `recipient_not_found`, and email fallback, if configured, runs straight away with the XML attached. Any other lookup problem, such as a directory that does not answer, does not count as not registered: the transmission is attempted as usual, and the fallback only runs if that attempt fails.

### What you need to do

Nothing, unless one of these applies:

- **You already pass `doctypeId` on email-only sends**, for example the default Peppol BIS 3 identifier. Those documents are now validated, and the email carries the XML as well. A buyer without a VAT number or enterprise number gets a `400` for Peppol BIS 3: add the number, or leave `doctypeId` out to keep sending a PDF only.
- **You react to delivery failure categories.** A send to an address that is not on Peppol now fails with `recipient_not_found` instead of `transport`. Without email delivery the response stays a `422` with the same message prefix; the detail now says the recipient is not registered.

The [email delivery guide](/docs/email-delivery-and-notifications#structured-documents-by-email) and the [German getting started guide](/getting-started/germany) describe both ways to reach German buyers that are not on Peppol.
