You can now pay for your Recommand subscription yearly and get **12 months for the price of 10**. That is two months free, every year. Annual plans are available on every plan with a fixed fee.

### What changed

- **Annual plans with 2 months free**: Pay for the year up front at the price of 10 months. Your annual price is locked in for the whole contract, so a later price change doesn't affect it. Usage above your allowance is still invoiced monthly at your plan's rate. We remind you 60 and 30 days before your plan renews.
- **Subscription fee billed in advance**: Each monthly invoice now covers the fixed fee for the month ahead rather than the month just passed. Usage above your monthly allowance is still invoiced once the month is closed. Your plan, allowance and prices stay the same.
- **Fairer plan changes**: Upgrades take effect immediately. The unused days of your current plan are credited and you pay only the difference for the remaining days. On a monthly plan, downgrades start the next month and cancellations take effect at the end of the current month. On an annual plan, they take effect at renewal.
- **Quote before you confirm**: Before you confirm a plan change, the subscription page shows what will be invoiced now and what will appear on your next invoice.

### One transition invoice

The invoice dated 30 September 2026 contains two subscription lines: the fee for September, which was due in any case, and the fee for October, now billed in advance. After that there is one subscription line per invoice. You aren't paying for anything twice; the next month's fee is invoiced one month earlier.

### Get started

Monthly billing remains the default and has no commitment. If you plan to use Recommand for the coming year, switching to yearly is the easiest way to save: open [Billing → Subscription](https://app.recommand.eu/billing/subscription) in the dashboard and choose annual billing.

Questions about the annual plan, your invoice or the transition? Contact [support@recommand.eu](mailto:support@recommand.eu).
