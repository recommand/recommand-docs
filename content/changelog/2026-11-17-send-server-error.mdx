---
title: A send that fails on Recommand's side answers 500
description: "A send that fails on Recommand's side before the document went out now answers `500` instead of `400`."
date: 2026-11-17
---

A send could fail because of a problem on Recommand's side, such as a lost database connection. It answered `400` with the error's own message, which looked like a problem with your request, so you might have changed a document that was fine.

### What changed

When such a failure happens before the document was handed to Peppol or email, nothing went out. The [send document endpoint](/reference/sending/send-document) now answers `500` with "Failed to send document", without the internal error message.

An error that says what to correct, such as a company without a sending identifier, still answers `400` with its message.

### What you need to do

Nothing. If you handle a `500` from the send endpoint, you can send the same document again, since nothing went out.
