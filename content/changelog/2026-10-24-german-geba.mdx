---
title: German companies are registered under their GEBA
description: German companies are now also registered under their German Electronic Business Address (GEBA, scheme 0246), the address the German identifier guideline recommends senders look a business up under. They keep sending from their VAT number.
date: 2026-10-24
---

The German identifier guideline (KoSIT, Germany Identifier Guideline v1.3) recommends that senders look a private German business up under its German Electronic Business Address (GEBA, scheme `0246`). The value of a GEBA is the business identification number (W-IdNr), which is the same as the business's VAT number. Until now, German companies were registered only under their VAT number with scheme `9930`. A sender whose software looked them up under `0246` did not find them.

### What changed

- **Registered under both schemes**: A new German company with a VAT number gets two identifiers: `9930:DE123456788` and `0246:DE123456788`. Both are registered on Peppol when the company is registered as a recipient, and moving a company to Germany adds both.
- **Existing German companies**: Existing German companies get the GEBA added as well, including companies that already have a GEBA with a distinctive feature or sub-address. Their own GEBAs stay as they are.
- **Sending is unchanged**: A German company keeps sending from `9930:` and its VAT number. The GEBA only adds an address it can receive on.
- **Never a reason to be refused**: When another provider already publishes the company's GEBA, the company is registered without it. Look the GEBA up with the [verify recipient endpoint](/reference/recipients/verify-recipient) to find that provider, and move it over with a [migration key](/docs/managing-companies#moving-from-another-provider-with-a-migration-key).
- **Adding it yourself**: Adding the plain GEBA with the [create company identifier endpoint](/reference/company-identifiers/create-company-identifier) returns the identifier the company already has instead of an error. GEBAs with a distinctive feature or sub-address, such as `0246:DE123456788-00001`, are still added that way.

### What you need to do

Nothing. If you list a German company's identifiers, expect a `0246` identifier next to the `9930` one. To send to a German business under its GEBA, use `0246:` followed by its VAT number as the recipient. The [German getting started guides](/getting-started/germany) have the details.
