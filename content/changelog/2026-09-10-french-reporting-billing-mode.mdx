---
title: Cross-border e-reporting now requires the invoicing framework
description: A French cross-border invoice report has to name its billingMode, and corrections that reuse an earlier reference now say so in the response.
date: 2026-09-10
---

A French cross-border invoice report now has to name the **invoicing framework**
of the document it reports, in the `billingMode` field. This is a breaking
change to the request contract of
`POST /:companyId/reporting/fr/b2bi` for `type: "invoice"`, and it is
deliberate: the framework decides how the tax administration reads the invoice,
and it cannot be derived from the figures on a report.

### What changed

- **`billingMode` is required on cross-border invoice reports.** It uses the
  same codes and the same meaning as the `billingMode` of a French regulated
  invoice: `B1`, `S1` and `M1` for a goods, services or mixed invoice, `B2`,
  `S2` and `M2` when the invoice was already paid, and `B4`, `S4` and `M4` for
  a final invoice after an advance payment. Other codes an invoice can carry
  are not supported on a report today and are refused with a 400. B2C reports
  and cross-border payment reports are unchanged.
- **Reports already on file stay readable.** Reports filed before this change
  carry no framework. They are returned, rendered and downloaded as before, and
  no framework is inferred for them.
- **A reused reference now says what happened.** The reference is the
  idempotency key, so sending a correction or a cancellation under the
  reference of the report it means to act on returns that earlier report with
  `duplicate: true` and files nothing. The response now also carries an
  optional `warning` describing what was found on file, so a correction that
  did nothing is visible straight away instead of at the end of the period. A
  plain retry of an identical request carries no warning.
- **The guide names the matching rules.** A correction or a cancellation is
  matched on the operation and never on the reference: a B2C sales report on
  the day, category and currency, a B2C payment report on the day and currency,
  a cross-border invoice on its document number, and a cross-border payment on
  the invoice number it belongs to. Dates are not part of the cross-border
  match. A correction replaces the report in full, and both a correction and a
  cancellation are refused once the filing for that period has been assembled,
  which is later than `periodEnd`.
- **Outcome codes are documented.** `outcomeCode` answers about the filing
  rather than about one report, so every report on the same filing shows the
  same code: `300` déposée, `500` recevable, `501` irrecevable. Unknown codes
  are passed through unchanged.

### What you need to do

Add `billingMode` to every cross-border invoice report you send, taking it from
the invoice. If you also send French regulated invoices through Recommand, it
is the value you already put on the invoice. Nothing changes for B2C reports,
for cross-border payment reports, or for reports you filed earlier.

The e-reporting section of the [French getting started
guides](/getting-started/france) lists the frameworks, the matching rules and
the outcome codes.
