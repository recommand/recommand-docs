---
title: Billing periods on documents and lines
description: Invoices and credit notes can now state the period they bill for, on the document and on each line, for example the month of a subscription.
date: 2026-10-15
---

Invoices and credit notes often bill for a period of time, such as a month of a subscription or a quarter of rent. You can now state that period on the document and on each line with the new `billingPeriod` field, and read it from documents you receive.

### What changed

- **New `billingPeriod` field**: An optional `billingPeriod` with a `startDate` and `endDate` (`YYYY-MM-DD`) on invoices, credit notes, self-billing invoices and self-billing credit notes. It is the invoicing period (BG-14) of EN 16931.
- **Billing periods on lines**: Lines take the same `billingPeriod`, the invoice line period (BG-26), so each line can state the period it covers, for example when one invoice bills several subscriptions.
- **Every format**: The period is written to and read from UBL as `cac:InvoicePeriod` and CII as `ram:BillingSpecifiedPeriod`, so Peppol BIS 3, XRechnung, the French formats and Factur-X all carry it.
- **Shown in the PDF**: The generated PDF shows the document period in the header and the line period under each line.

### Validation

A period needs at least a `startDate` or an `endDate`, and the `endDate` may not be before the `startDate`. When the document has a billing period, the period of each line must fall within it (PEPPOL-EN16931-R110 and R111). A document that breaks these rules is refused before anything is sent; for a line outside the document period, the error names the line.

### Example

```json
{
  "invoiceNumber": "INV-2025-0042",
  "billingPeriod": {
    "startDate": "2025-03-01",
    "endDate": "2025-03-31"
  },
  "lines": [
    {
      "name": "Pro plan subscription",
      "netPriceAmount": "49.00",
      "billingPeriod": {
        "startDate": "2025-03-01",
        "endDate": "2025-03-31"
      }
    }
  ]
}
```

### What you need to do

Nothing: `billingPeriod` is optional, and documents without it are sent as before. If your invoices bill for a period, add it so the buyer's software can book the cost in the right period.
