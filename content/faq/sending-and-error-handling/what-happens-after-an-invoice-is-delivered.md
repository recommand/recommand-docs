---
title: What happens after an invoice is successfully delivered?
category: Sending & error handling
excerpt: The recipient's access point has acknowledged the document; software processing follows.
updatedAt: 2026-09-14
---

For Peppol, a `delivered` status means the recipient's access point acknowledged the document. The recipient's software then processes it according to its own workflow; delivery does not guarantee immediate visibility, approval or payment.

See [Tracking Delivery](/docs/email-delivery-and-notifications#tracking-delivery) to distinguish send acceptance from confirmed delivery.
