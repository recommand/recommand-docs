## Verify the company

A company cannot exchange documents on Peppol until an authorised representative
has confirmed their identity. The company object exposes this as `isVerified`,
and it stays `false` until the check is done.

For Belgian companies the flow is short:

1. Open the verification URL (from the create-company response, the dashboard, or
   a fresh one from the [verify company
   endpoint](/reference/companies/verify-company)).
2. Fill in the representative's first and last name. That name is checked
   against the **Crossroads Bank for Enterprises** before the
   identity check starts: a name that matches none of the company's registered
   representatives is refused.
3. Complete the identity check. `isVerified` flips to `true` and the company is
   published on the Peppol network.

In some cases manual verification by our team will be required.
If that's the case, `isVerified` will remain `false` until this manual verification is completed.
The user is informed of this in the verification process.
