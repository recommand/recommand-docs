## Verify the company

A company cannot exchange documents on Peppol until an authorised representative
has confirmed their identity. The company object exposes this as `isVerified`,
and it stays `false` until the check is done.

For Norwegian companies, the representative's authority is held against
Enhetsregisteret:

1. Open the verification URL (from the create-company response, the dashboard, or
   a fresh one from the [verify company
   endpoint](/reference/companies/verify-company)).
2. Pick your name from the list. The list shows the people Enhetsregisteret names
   for signature (signatur) or procuration (prokura). Someone who is not on it
   cannot verify the company and is asked to contact
   [support@recommand.eu](mailto:support@recommand.eu). When the register lists
   nobody, the representative types their name instead, and it is checked the same
   way.
3. Complete the identity check. When the representative may act for the company
   alone, `isVerified` flips to `true` and the company is published in ELMA.

Of the people on the list, a representative may act alone when the register says
they can sign or hold procuration on their own, or when they hold a role with full
rights, such as
general manager (daglig leder), chair of the board (styrets leder) or owner of a
sole proprietorship (innehaver).

### When the register cannot settle it

Some cases need a person to look at them, for example a board member who may only
sign together with others, or a register entry that cannot be interpreted. The
identity check still runs, and the verification is then held for review by our
team: `isVerified` stays `false` until it is decided, and the representative is
told so during the verification.
