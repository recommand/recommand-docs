### Moving an existing registration

Recipient registration is exclusive everywhere: a company that already receives
through another Peppol provider has to be deregistered there before it can be
registered with Recommand.

If you do not know who the current provider is, look the company up as a
recipient: the [verify endpoint](/reference/recipients/verify-recipient) returns
the SMP it is published on, which names the provider to ask.
