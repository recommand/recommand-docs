---
title: Recipient registration in Germany
---

### Moving an existing German registration

Recipient registration is exclusive: a German company that already receives
through another Peppol provider has to be deregistered there before it can be
registered with Recommand, unless that provider issues a
[migration key](/docs/managing-companies#moving-from-another-provider-with-a-migration-key),
which moves the registration over without a gap.

If you do not know who the current provider is, look the VAT number up as a
recipient (`9930:DE123456788`): the [verify
endpoint](/reference/recipients/verify-recipient) returns the SMP it is
published on, which names the provider to ask.

### The GEBA

A German company is registered under its GEBA (`0246:DE123456788`) next to its
VAT number. When another provider already publishes the GEBA, the company is
not refused: it is registered without the GEBA, which then no longer appears
among its identifiers. Look `0246:` and the VAT number up the same way to find
that provider. Add the GEBA again with that provider's [migration
key](/docs/managing-companies#moving-from-another-provider-with-a-migration-key)
to move it over, or once the provider has deregistered it.
