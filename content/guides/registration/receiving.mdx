---
title: Registration for receiving
---

### Registering as a recipient

To receive documents, the company must be published as a recipient on an SMP (Service Metadata Publisher).
That is what `isSmpRecipient` does, and it is the default:

```json
{
  "isSmpRecipient": true
}
```

What that means:

- The company becomes findable on the Peppol network: any sender can look it up
  and deliver to it via the Peppol network.
- Recipient registration is **exclusive**. If the company is already registered
  for receiving through another Peppol provider, registration fails until it is
  deregistered there, or until you hand us a migration key from that provider
  (see below).
- The document types the company accepts are registered along with it, based on
  its country. Which ones those are is covered further down.
- The company is only published once it is verified.

### Moving from another provider with a migration key

Deregistering at the previous provider and registering with Recommand leaves a
gap during which nobody can deliver to the company. The Peppol network has a
way around that: the current provider asks the network to prepare the move and
receives a one-time **migration key**, which the company passes on to its new
provider. The new provider claims the registration with that key, and the
network switches the company over in one step.

Ask the previous provider for a migration key for the exact identifier that is
registered with them, for example `0208:1012081766`. Then submit it with the
[migrate company identifier endpoint](/reference/company-identifiers/migrate-company-identifier):

```json
POST /api/v1/companies/{companyId}/identifiers/{identifierId}/migration
{
  "migrationKey": "Ab12$#xyZ9!kLm"
}
```

If the identifier does not exist on the company yet, add it with the
[create company identifier endpoint](/reference/company-identifiers/create-company-identifier)
and pass the `migrationKey` in the same request.

What happens next depends on whether the company can be published already:

- A company that is verified is taken over immediately. The response has
  status `completed`, and the network routes to Recommand within minutes.
- A company whose identity check is still open keeps the key with status
  `pending`. The moment the company is verified and registered, the key is used.
  Check the outcome with the
  [list company identifier migrations endpoint](/reference/company-identifiers/get-company-identifier-migrations).
- A key the network refuses ends in status `failed` with the reason. The usual
  causes are a key issued for a different identifier, a copy error, or a
  previous provider that cancelled the migration. Ask them for a fresh key and
  submit it again; a newer key replaces a pending one.

Migration keys are not available for companies published through a partner
SMP, which today means companies registered in France.

<Callout type="info" title="Leaving Recommand">
  The same mechanism works the other way. Mail
  [support@recommand.eu](mailto:support@recommand.eu) with the company and the
  identifier to move, and we issue a migration key for your new provider. The
  company keeps receiving through Recommand until that provider claims it.
</Callout>
