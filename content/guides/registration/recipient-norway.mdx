---
title: Recipient registration in Norway
---

### Registration in ELMA

A Norwegian company registered as a recipient is published in ELMA under its
organisation number, with the processes its document types belong to, and with
its name and contact details (name, email address and phone number) as the
participant's contact. When the company's name, email address or phone number
changes, the ELMA entry is updated with it.

Senders find the company the usual way, and the [verify
endpoint](/reference/recipients/verify-recipient) finds it shortly after it is
registered.

On the test network, Norwegian companies are registered in ELMA's test
environment in the same way.

### Moving an existing Norwegian registration

Recipient registration is exclusive. How a Norwegian company that already
receives elsewhere moves to Recommand depends on where it is registered:

- **In ELMA, through another service provider.** When the verified company is
  registered, Recommand asks ELMA to transfer the participant to it. If ELMA does
  not allow the transfer, the registration fails with a message that names the
  organisation number, and the current provider has to transfer it to Recommand
  in ELMA.
- **On another SMP.** The company has to be deregistered there first. ELMA does
  not take over participants with SML [migration
  keys](/docs/managing-companies#moving-from-another-provider-with-a-migration-key),
  so they do not apply to Norwegian companies.

If you do not know who the current provider is, look the organisation number up
as a recipient (`0192:123456785`): the [verify
endpoint](/reference/recipients/verify-recipient) returns the SMP it is published
on.
