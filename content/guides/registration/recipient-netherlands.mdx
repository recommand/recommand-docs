### Moving an existing Dutch registration

Dutch companies are commonly already reachable over Peppol, because e-invoicing
towards Dutch central government has been the norm for years and accounting
software often registers the company on the sender's behalf.

There is no automatic migration for the Netherlands: the company has to be
deregistered at its current provider before it can be registered here. If you do
not know who that provider is, look the KVK number up as a recipient — the
[verify endpoint](/reference/recipients/verify-recipient) returns the SMP the
company is published on.
