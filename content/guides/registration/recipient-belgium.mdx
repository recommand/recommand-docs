### Moving an existing Belgian registration

Since the B2B mandate came into force, most Belgian companies are already
reachable over Peppol through a provider of their own. Recipient registration is
exclusive, so registering the enterprise number under scheme `0208` fails while
the company is still published somewhere else: it has to be deregistered at its
current provider first.

If this is the case, we will let you know which SMP the company is published on.
You can also look the enterprise number up ahead of time
with the [verify endpoint](/reference/recipients/verify-recipient), which returns
the same thing.

To move without a gap in reception, ask the current provider for a migration key
and submit it as described in
[Moving from another provider with a migration key](/docs/managing-companies#moving-from-another-provider-with-a-migration-key).
Not every Belgian provider issues one; if yours does not, deregistering there
remains the way.

<Callout type="info" title="If the answer is not obvious">
  Mail [support@recommand.eu](mailto:support@recommand.eu) with the enterprise
  number and we will work out where the company is registered.
</Callout>
