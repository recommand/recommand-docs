## Where to get help

- **Troubleshooting.** Common rejections, delivery failures and validation errors
  are collected in the [troubleshooting guide](/docs/troubleshooting-guide).
- **Questions.** The [FAQ](/faq) covers Peppol, addressing, VAT and billing.
- **Email.** [support@recommand.eu](mailto:support@recommand.eu):include the
  company ID and, for a delivery problem, the document ID.
- **Discord.** [Join the server](https://discord.gg/a2tcQYA3ew) for release
  announcements and quick questions.
- **Keep track of changes.** New endpoints and behaviour are recorded in the
  [changelog](/changelog).
