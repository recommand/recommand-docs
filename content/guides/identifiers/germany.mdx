---

### Three identifiers that are easy to mix up

A German invoice involves three different identifiers. Only the first one is a
Peppol address:

| Identifier                   | What it is for                                                   | Where it goes                                  |
| ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Participant identifier       | The company's address on the Peppol network                      | A company identifier, e.g. `9930:DE123456788` or `0246:DE123456788` |
| Legal registration identifier | The register entry, e.g. `HRB 12345 B`, shown on the invoice (BT-30) | `enterpriseNumber`, with an optional `enterpriseNumberScheme` |
| Buyer reference (BT-10)      | The buyer's routing reference; for an authority, its Leitweg-ID   | `buyerReference` on the document               |

### German identifiers and Peppol address

| Field                    | German value                                          |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| `country`                | `"DE"`                                                |
| `vatNumber`              | `DE` + 9 digits, e.g. `DE123456788`                   |
| `enterpriseNumber`       | Optional: the commercial register number              |
| `enterpriseNumberScheme` | Optional: leave it out unless the buyer expects one   |

```json title="company.json"
{
  "name": "Beispiel GmbH",
  "address": "Musterstraße 1",
  "postalCode": "10115",
  "city": "Berlin",
  "country": "DE",
  "enterpriseNumber": "HRB 12345 B",
  "vatNumber": "DE123456788"
}
```

The VAT number is checked against the German format and its check digit, and
registered as two Peppol identifiers:

- **`9930:` followed by the VAT number**, e.g. `9930:DE123456788`
- **`0246:` followed by the VAT number**, e.g. `0246:DE123456788`: the
  company's German Electronic Business Address (GEBA). Its value is the
  business identification number (W-IdNr), which is the same as the VAT number.

The German identifier guideline recommends the GEBA as the first address a
sender looks a German business up under, so with both registered the company is
found whichever of the two a sender uses. The register number is never
registered as a Peppol identifier.

### More GEBAs: distinctive feature and sub-address

A GEBA can extend the W-IdNr with a hyphen and a 5 digit distinctive feature
(Unterscheidungsmerkmal), and with a full stop and a sub-address of up to 8
letters or digits, for example `0246:DE123456788-00001` or
`0246:DE123456788-00001.EINKAUF`. Add these yourself with the [create
identifier endpoint](/reference/company-identifiers/create-company-identifier)
or in the dashboard. A GEBA always has to start with the company's own VAT
number. Adding the plain `0246:` VAT number again returns the identifier the
company already has.

### When the VAT number changes

Identifiers are not rewritten when you change a company's VAT number or remove
it: the `9930` and `0246` identifiers stay as they were registered. Update or
remove them with the [company identifier
endpoints](/reference/company-identifiers/create-company-identifier). Moving a
company to another country removes both and adds that country's default
identifiers.

### Without a VAT number

A company without a VAT number gets no identifier by default. Following the
German identifier guideline, add one of these yourself with the [create
identifier endpoint](/reference/company-identifiers/create-company-identifier)
or in the dashboard:

- `0088`: the company's GS1 Global Location Number (GLN), 13 digits
- `9918`: the company's IBAN

Both are checked for their check digits.

### Public authorities: the Leitweg-ID

A German public authority receives invoices under scheme `0204` with its
Leitweg-ID, for example `0204:991-33333TEST-33`. Only add a `0204` identifier
for a public authority that receives through Recommand, using the exact
Leitweg-ID it was assigned; its check digits are verified. The older Leitweg-ID
scheme `9958` is deprecated on Peppol and is refused.

### Which identifier you send from

A company sends from the identifier with the lowest scheme, but never from a
Leitweg-ID: that one only names an authority's invoice reception. A GEBA is
passed over as well while the company has its VAT number under `9930`, so a
German company keeps sending from `9930:` and its VAT number. An invoice to
a Leitweg-ID always goes out under the company's VAT number (`9930`) or, without
one, its GLN (`0088`), the sender identifiers the federal invoice portals list.
