## Report B2C transactions

Invoices cover business customers within France. For sales to private individuals or international B2B customers, France
expects daily totals instead, and Recommand files them for you through `POST
/:companyId/reporting/fr/b2c`. You never build a regulatory file yourself.

```json
{
  "reference": "B2C-SALES-2026-08-17-GOODS",
  "type": "sales",
  "date": "2026-08-17",
  "category": "goods",
  "currency": "EUR",
  "taxExclusiveAmount": "10000.00",
  "taxAmount": "2000.00",
  "transactionCount": 42,
  "vatBreakdown": [
    { "percentage": "20.00", "taxableAmount": "10000.00", "taxAmount": "2000.00" }
  ]
}
```

- **Sales reports** (`type: "sales"`) carry the normal daily totals, one per day
  and category, regardless of when customers pay. Taxable goods and taxable
  services are supported.
- **Payment reports** (`type: "payment"`) are an extra report for services on
  cash-basis VAT (*TVA sur les encaissements*), submitted for the day the payment
  is received. Do not send them for goods, or for services where VAT is due on
  invoicing (*TVA sur les débits*).
- **`reference` makes retries safe.** Reuse it only to retry the exact same
  request; use a new one, together with the optional `action` field, to correct or
  cancel an earlier daily report.

A submitted report is stored alongside your sent documents and counts towards your
document quota.

<Callout type="info" title="Only for French companies, API only">
  B2C reporting is available for companies registered in France and needs a valid
  nine-digit SIREN on the company. It is submitted through the API; if you need it
  without building an integration, contact
  [support@recommand.eu](mailto:support@recommand.eu).
</Callout>
