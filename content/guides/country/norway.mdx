## Peppol in Norway

Norway is one of the oldest Peppol countries: public sector buyers have required
EHF invoices for years, and EHF Billing 3.0 is Peppol BIS Billing 3.0. Documents
are exchanged as **Peppol BIS 3 UBL** over the standard Peppol billing process,
through Recommand's own access point.

What is specific to Norway:

- **A B2B mandate in two steps.** From 1 January 2027, businesses with a
  bookkeeping obligation must issue e-invoices to other such businesses. Until
  receiving becomes mandatory as well, on 1 January 2030, the obligation in
  practice applies when the buyer can receive them, which means being registered
  in ELMA or on Peppol.
- **Receivers are registered in ELMA.** ELMA (Elektronisk mottakeradresseregister)
  is the Norwegian receiver register, run by the Norwegian Digitalisation Agency.
  Norwegian companies that receive through Recommand are published there rather
  than on Recommand's own SMP, and ELMA keeps their entries in the Peppol SML and
  the Peppol Directory up to date. Senders find them the usual way, through the SML.
- **The organisation number is the Peppol address.** Norwegian companies are
  published under scheme `0192` with their 9-digit organisasjonsnummer, so a
  Norwegian Peppol address looks like `0192:123456785`.
- **Organisations are checked against Enhetsregisteret.** An organisation number
  that the Norwegian register of legal entities does not know, or has deleted,
  is not registered as a receiver, and a registration is removed when the
  organisation is deleted from the register later.
- **Representatives are checked for signing authority.** During verification, the
  representative is held against the people Enhetsregisteret lists as able to
  sign for the company or holding procuration.
